## Why a leaf, given the design system's "no leaf" rule

`design_handoff_bonbibi_panel/README.md` §11 lists a generic leaf among
forbidden decorative motifs for Bonbibi's brand mark (alongside swoosh,
droplet, orb, gradient blob). That rule targets generic, meaningless
decoration substituting for the mark. This leaf is used deliberately: it
represents the Sundarbans mangrove forest itself — the setting Bonbibi
guards, not the figure of Bonbibi — and its blocky pixel-grid construction
matches the panel's own integer-pixel, Jersey-grid aesthetic. Intentional
override, not an oversight; the "datum-and-risers" mark (README §4) remains
the primary in-panel safety-context mark. The leaf is the brand/presentation
mark: wordmark accent, favicon, video and submission materials.
